Key Tips for Beginners:
Start early and maintain a daily study routine. Focus on concept clarity rather than rote learning. Prioritize subjects based on your strengths and UPSC weightage. Stay updated with current affairs and integrate them into Mains answers. Take mock tests regularly to improve speed and accuracy. By following a structured approach, leveraging quality resources, and maintaining consistent effort, beginners can gradually build the foundation needed to succeed in the IAS exam
